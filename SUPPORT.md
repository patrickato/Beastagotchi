# Support and Bug Reports

Beastagotchi is pre-1.0 software and currently targets technically comfortable testers.

## Before filing an issue

1. Read `docs/INSTALLATION.md` and `docs/TESTING.md`.
2. Confirm which Beastagotchi version/commit you are running.
3. Note your Pi model, OS/Pwnagotchi version, display/touch hardware and relevant optional hardware.
4. Reproduce the issue after a clean service restart if safe to do so.
5. Sanitize all logs/support material before posting it publicly.

## Useful evidence

Good bug reports usually include:

- expected behavior;
- actual behavior;
- exact reproduction steps;
- service status/health summary;
- sanitized logs around the event;
- whether the failure survives reboot/restart;
- whether Pwnagotchi/Bettercap remain healthy;
- screenshots or photos for UI/display problems;
- whether the issue occurs off-screen, on physical TFT, or both.

## Do not post publicly

Do not post credentials, raw captures, private SSIDs/BSSIDs/client identifiers, exact private GPS traces, personal configuration or unsanitized Beast support bundles.

For security-sensitive bugs, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a detailed public issue.
