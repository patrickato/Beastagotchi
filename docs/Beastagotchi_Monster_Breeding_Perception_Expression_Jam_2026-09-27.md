# Beastagotchi Monster / Breeding / Perception / Expression Jam — 2026-09-27

Status: candidate design discussion before final reconciliation. Nothing here overrides existing deterministic Beast/Doctor truth.

## Core separation

1. Machine capability is never gated by breeding/progression. Hardware and software abilities remain owner capabilities.
2. Perception is what Beast can truthfully sense: radio, GPS, time, environment, system health, camera/mic when present, peers, power, etc.
3. Interpretation is the creature layer: traits/instincts/affinities determine what the creature notices, values, remembers, fears, seeks, or reacts strongly to.
4. Expression is how the creature reacts through available outputs: TFT animation/text, LEDs, sound, haptics, phone, WebUI, etc.
5. AI may enrich wording/personality/expression but cannot invent facts, progression, captures, events, lineage, or rewards.

## Creature identity and progression

6. Creature identity should derive from a deterministic seed/profile plus history, not be regenerated by AI.
7. Growth should reflect real use and meaningful events rather than idle uptime alone.
8. Core progression should be broad enough for long-term ownership and not force a single optimal playstyle.
9. Different activity patterns can produce different affinities without locking out other machine functions.
10. Traits should influence interpretation/behavior/expression, not fundamental Linux/Pwnagotchi capability.
11. Example trait families: curious, cautious, social, nocturnal, storm-sensitive, radio-obsessed, explorer, tinkerer, resilient, collector, analytical.
12. Traits can have strengths/weaknesses/tendencies rather than binary classes.
13. Major milestones may create visible evolution/morph states, but cosmetic/behavioral change should be separable from hard capability.

## Breeding / lineage

14. Breeding combines lineage traits, tendencies, expression styles, affinities, and rare inherited quirks.
15. Breeding should not be a mandatory progression path.
16. Inheritance can mix deterministic parent traits with bounded randomness so offspring are not clones.
17. Rare recessive-like traits and combination traits are good candidates.
18. Lineage history should remain inspectable: parents, ancestry, inherited traits, mutations/rare events, notable milestones.
19. Cross-device breeding/lineage exchange can be possible later without requiring a central service.
20. The reward for deeper/rarer discoveries can scale substantially, including increasing odds of other rare outcomes, but exact economy values remain an owner-design decision later.

## Perception

21. New hardware expands senses, not the creature's legal permission to use the machine.
22. GPS can become spatial awareness; SDR can become RF awareness; environmental sensors can become temperature/pressure/etc awareness; camera/mic can become optional later senses.
23. Perception must preserve source/freshness/trust. The creature can react to uncertainty but must not present uncertain data as fact.
24. Repeated environments can become familiar places/patterns to the creature without exposing private detail publicly.
25. Time/celestial/seasonal context can influence behavior and rare reactions when based on real time/location data.
26. Pwnagotchi-specific activity should remain central: AP density, peers, captures/handshakes, epochs, channels, sessions, etc. can feed creature state.
27. System health can also be perceived: heat, low power, storage pressure, failures, recovery, long quiet periods.

## Expression

28. Expression is semantic first: e.g. curious, excited, wary, tired, proud, startled, sick, focused, affectionate, triumphant.
29. Available hardware maps that semantic state to outputs; lack of LEDs/audio/haptics never breaks the underlying reaction.
30. Expression should vary enough to feel alive but stay grounded in actual state/events.
31. Creature reactions should sometimes be subtle; constant animation/noise would become annoying.
32. Rare events deserve qualitatively stronger expression than ordinary events.
33. Different lineages/traits can express the same underlying event differently.
34. Owner should be able to tone down, mute, or disable expression channels independently.
35. AI can add richer dialogue/narration when available; deterministic fallback lines/animations must still exist without AI.

## Memory / relationship

36. Creature memory should be structured Beast data, not model memory.
37. Candidate memory types: notable firsts, rare events, favorite places/contexts, repeated hardware companions, peers, owner-created notes, major repairs/recoveries, lineage events.
38. Creature should not remember every trivial event forever; significance rules and bounded summaries matter.
39. Relationship with owner should grow from real interaction/use, not arbitrary daily-login streaks.
40. The creature may develop preferences/tendencies, but owner control always wins.

## Rare events / easter eggs

41. Hidden events should be layered by rarity/depth, with deeper discoveries yielding meaningfully larger rewards or odds shifts.
42. Triggers should be based on real interactions/events and/or deliberate hidden sequences, not accidental destructive behavior.
43. Rare events can affect lineage, morphology, expression, traits, discovery odds, collectibles, Chronicle/achievements, or future breeding outcomes.
44. Some rare outcomes should be globally rare; others may be hardware/context-specific.
45. Secrets/easter eggs should not gate essential machine functions.

## Interaction model

46. Creature and Doctor are distinct roles but can coexist. Doctor is the competent companion/assistant; the creature is the living identity/personality layer.
47. The creature can point toward Doctor when something is wrong without pretending to diagnose it itself.
48. Example: creature looks ill/tired because thermal/power evidence is bad; tapping the state opens Doctor evidence.
49. Conversely, successful repairs can become creature memories/milestones/recovery reactions.
50. Homecoming can combine factual session truth with creature reaction/narration; AI can enrich presentation but facts stay deterministic.

## Recommended architectural rule

> Beast senses truthfully. The creature interprets those senses through traits, memory, lineage, and context, then expresses the interpretation through whatever outputs exist. Breeding changes interpretation and expression, not fundamental machine capability.
