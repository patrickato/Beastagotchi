# SPOILERS — Beastagotchi Secrets & Achievement Systems

This document intentionally reveals systems that can otherwise be discovered organically.

## Rare Moments

Beastagotchi can schedule an extremely small number of rare event windows per year. The schedule is unique to the local Beast and is not shown in the normal UI. If the device is off throughout a window, that opportunity is missed.

Before a Rare Moment, translucent sigils may fade over the normal interface. During the event, touching the active cinematic counts as a deliberate witness acknowledgement. The `I Saw It` achievement is tied to that acknowledgement rather than merely to the event being rendered.

## Achievement families

The catalog includes long-tail milestones for lifetime signal discovery, vendor/species discovery, GPS use, capture cataloging, runtime, evolution levels and rare events. Future catalogs add hardware, themes, modes, sessions, seasonal/celestial events and dedicated Cipher Console secrets.

Rarity tiers are Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic.

## Future Cipher Console

The planned Cipher Console deliberately isolates secret codes from normal touch gestures. It can present a number pad, phone-pattern grid, retro D-pad/A/B controller, symbol pad or puzzle surface. This is where sequence-based easter eggs belong.

More exact secret triggers will be added here as they become production features. This file is the canonical spoiler location for the eventual public GitHub release.

## v0.9.5 — Rare Event presentation families

A Rare Moment now includes a presentation family in addition to its sigil and rarity. Current families are:

- `fade` — stationary materialization/fade;
- `drift` — slow movement while fading;
- `cross` — traverses the display;
- `orbit` — curved/orbiting motion;
- `ghost` — subdued apparition-like movement;
- `storm` — a denser field reaction;
- `apparition` — a more explicit manifestation;
- `cinematic` — heavier procedural placeholder for the future pre-rendered cinematic path.

These are presentation mechanics, not the final Legendary/Mythic art assets. Preview commands are intentionally safe and do not consume a scheduled event.

## v0.9.5 — Achievement Explorer

The on-device explorer now reveals normal achievement metadata including rarity, category, progress, reward XP and lock state. Dedicated hidden achievements may still suppress their identity/description until the design calls for disclosure. Awards are tracked separately so evolution forms, auras and other reward classes do not have to masquerade as ordinary achievements.

The eventual public spoiler documentation should list every production trigger/unlock/reward, even when the normal interface intentionally keeps it secret. Users who want discovery can avoid this file; builders and completionists have a canonical source.
