# Beastagotchi v0.19 — First Five Experience Home Proofs
## 2026-09-24

Status: off-screen prototype evidence only. Not physical TFT acceptance and not final art.

## Purpose

This checkpoint proves that Experience DNA can create materially different product languages instead of merely recoloring one dashboard.

All five Home proofs render from the same sanitized real target state fixture.

## Atlas

Reads as a field/expedition instrument.

- dominant spatial field-context canvas;
- actual RF observations are projected into a diagram explicitly labelled non-geographic;
- GPS SEARCHING is shown because the fixture has no fix and zero route points;
- no route is fabricated;
- Beast is a supporting field companion;
- live radio/discovery/journey/system facts stay at the edge.

Current fidelity update: the former four-card right-side stack has been replaced by a ruled field-notebook margin; the field canvas now bleeds across the body and the expedition ledger spans the page. Remaining work is source-art/environment depth and owner visual acceptance.

## Forge

Reads as an industrial machine bay.

- compute, radio and power occupy explicit subsystem modules;
- a machine bus and Beast Core connect the composition;
- I/O/capabilities and Doctor/Governor are operational subsystems;
- unavailable power telemetry is shown as NO SENSOR rather than invented;
- Doctor is visually native and prominent.

Current fidelity update: Home has been rebuilt as a single continuous chassis with integrated compute gauge, radio deck, power train, machine bus/Beast core, I/O ports and mounted Doctor/Governor fault module. It no longer uses the repeated rounded-card module layout. Material/texture remains intentionally restrained; owner visual acceptance is still pending.

## Observatory

Reads as a scientific measurement station.

- RF observation plot is built from the actual AP/channel/RSSI capture;
- tuned channel marker uses the real current channel;
- secondary plot is an actual observed-band distribution, not synthetic history;
- provenance panel identifies the captured-state source and GPS truth;
- footer explicitly says NO SYNTHETIC HISTORY;
- Beast is a small observer rather than the visual center.

Current fidelity update: Home now uses one continuous measurement surface with open scientific axes, an integrated observed-band comparison strip and a ruled provenance/observer margin instead of boxed plot/provenance panels. Larger-history/correlation surfaces still require genuine persisted history before they can be added.

## Habitat

Reads as a living companion environment.

- Beast dominates the scene;
- progression, mood and real relationship/context signals are subordinate;
- session discoveries, lifetime encounters and growth are environmental objects rather than dashboard cards;
- Expedition state appears as an ambient roaming cue;
- operational truth remains present in a small strip rather than reclaiming the screen.

Current fidelity update: the former circle/dot-eye mascot has been replaced by a layered organic companion portrait with body/shoulder depth, head/cheek planes, inner ears, expressive eyes, mood-driven mouth and a small collar identity tag. It remains procedural/project-owned proof art; richer Pack-ready creature/environment assets and behavior choreography are still future fidelity work.

## Monolith

Reads as a restrained premium product surface.

- large negative space;
- one focal creature;
- exactly one primary fact row;
- quiet contextual footer;
- no grid, scanline, particle field or dense instrumentation required;
- health is a tiny status cue unless intervention is needed.

Current fidelity update: the former circle-outline face has been replaced by a restrained sculptural/faceted focal Beast with subtle depth rings, eye slits and engraved identity while preserving the large negative-space and single-fact hierarchy. Typography, transition choreography and final Pack-ready focal art still determine whether the finished product reaches the full premium target.

## Result

The five prototypes are structurally and visually distinct before palette customization.

They differ in:
- dominant silhouette;
- content hierarchy;
- creature presence;
- information density;
- Doctor relationship;
- use of negative space;
- treatment of the same canonical telemetry.

This satisfies the first internal diversification proof, but not owner visual acceptance.
