# Beastagotchi Community Code of Conduct

Beastagotchi is intended to be a collaborative technical project. Contributors and testers are expected to treat one another with respect, assume good faith while discussing technical disagreements, and keep criticism focused on code, design, testing and documented behavior rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate exposure of another person's private network/location data, or sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Be specific, show evidence when possible, explain tradeoffs, and make room for maintainers/testers to reproduce a claim before treating it as settled.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions that violate these expectations or expose private/security-sensitive information.
